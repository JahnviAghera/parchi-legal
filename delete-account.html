Delete your Parchi account

You can delete your Parchi account and its personal data at any time. Use whichever way is easiest.

1. In the app

Open Parchi › Account (dealers) or Settings (factories) › Delete account › Yes, delete my account. It happens immediately.

2. By SMS, without the app

From the mobile number you use for Parchi, send the SMS PARCHI DELETE to +91 87994 48954. Sending it from your own number proves the request is yours. We delete the account within 30 days.

3. By email

Write to agherajahnvi@gmail.com with your Parchi mobile number. We may text that number to confirm it's you. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and mobile number
  • Your name and profile
  • Your place in any shop or factory team
  • Your notifications and the ID of your phone

What is kept

Orders, invoices, dispatch and payment records stay with the shop and the manufacturer involved, without your name on them, for as long as tax law requires businesses to keep such records. Encrypted backups that still contain your data are deleted within 35 days.

If you are the only owner of a factory on Parchi, add another owner first, or write to us to close the factory's account.
