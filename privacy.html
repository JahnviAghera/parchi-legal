Parchi

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Parchi is run by Aghera Jahnvi, India ("we"). This page says what personal data the Parchi app holds, why, and what you can do about it. Parchi is for people aged 18 or older who run or work for a business.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your mobile numberTo log you in and to connect you to your shop or factory.
Your name, shop or factory name, GSTIN, shop and delivery addressesSo orders, dispatches and invoices are made out correctly.
Orders, dispatch details, invoices, payment status and khata balanceThis is the service: a shared record between a dealer and their manufacturer.
Photos you attach to a problem reportTo show the manufacturer what went wrong.
A random ID for the phone you logged in on, and your languageTo keep one active phone per number, and to show the app in your language.
The login SMS you send us (your number and the text "PARCHI …")To prove the number is yours. Other messages on our login number are never read by Parchi.
The time you confirmed you are 18 or older and agreed to these termsTo keep a record of your consent.

What we don't collect

Your contacts, your location, your photos (other than ones you attach), card or bank details, or advertising IDs. Parchi has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers. When you pay an invoice, your UPI app handles the payment; Parchi never sees your bank details.

Who sees your data

  • The business you trade with. A dealer's shop details, orders and dues are visible to the manufacturer they order from, and that manufacturer's catalogue, prices and dispatch details are visible to the dealer. Team members of a shop or factory see what their role allows.
  • Our service providers. Data is stored with Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India. Encrypted backups are kept with GitHub. They process data only to run Parchi.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. When you do, your login, name, phone number, team memberships and notifications are removed. Orders, invoices and payments stay with the businesses involved, without your name on them, for as long as tax law requires those businesses to keep records. Backups are deleted after 35 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your data: most of it is in the app under Account. For anything else, write to us.
  • Delete your account: see Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account, or write to us.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act: write to us.
  • Complain: write to us first at agherajahnvi@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. The database enforces who can see what, so a shop sees only its own records and its own manufacturers' catalogues. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will ask you to agree again.
